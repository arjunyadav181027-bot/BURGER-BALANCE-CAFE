About Us

A short story of how BurgerBalanceCafe started.

Our Story

BurgerBalanceCafe started with one simple idea. Most fast food makes you choose between a burger you really want and a burger you feel okay about eating. We did not think that should be a hard choice.

So we made two menus in one restaurant. The Flame Side is for full flavour burgers with no changes. The Balance Side uses lighter ingredients and we print the calories on the menu so nothing is hidden.

No side is the good one or the bad one. Both are honest answers to the same question - what do you want to eat today?

Timeline

  • 2021 - Started as a single cart menu with one burger, the Balance Original.
  • 2022 - Added the Flame Side after customers asked for something heavier.
  • 2023 - Opened the first counter location with two menu boards.
  • 2024 - Started listing calories on every Balance Side item.

Our Values

Honesty

No hidden calories. What is written on the menu is what you get.

Choice

We do not rank one side above the other. You decide what you want.

Quality

Same kitchen and same standard for both sides of the menu.

Fairness

Portion sizes and prices are kept fair, with no forced upselling.
